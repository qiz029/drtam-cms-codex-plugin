# Support

For installation problems, CLI compatibility issues, or Plugin defects, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/qiz029/drtam-cms-codex-plugin/issues).

For Dr Tam CMS Device Access approval or revocation, contact the clinic
administrator. Administrators review pending requests at
<https://drtam-medspa-clinic.com/admin/device-code-request>.

Do not include Device Codes, credentials, patient information, private Media,
or other sensitive content in a public GitHub issue. Security and privacy
reports may be sent privately to <info@drtam-medspa.com>.
